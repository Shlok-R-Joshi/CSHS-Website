CS Wednesdays Volunteer Form

As a volunteer you may be selected to facilitate a discussion at our CS Wednesday events. You will receive service points depending on how many hours you spend on it (up to 8 hours total). If you would like you can do it with one or two other people.

You may use whatever you want to present your selected topic whether it is a slideshow or other format. Additionally, you must include an engaging activity to supplement your presentation. You will be notified at least one week in advance, and if you are unable to attend you must let one of the officers know ASAP.
